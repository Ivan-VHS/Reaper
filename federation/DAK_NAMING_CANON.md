# Dak Naming Canon — shared product identity

Dak is the operating-system name and the canonical system identity. Use `Dak` for user-facing references and `dak` / `dakos` only where a lowercase technical namespace is required by the owning contract.

Reaper, Pytyliki, OPlague and VentuSample remain independent product identities with their own responsibilities. Do not prepend `Dak` to their product names, package IDs or executable names unless a specific cross-system contract requires it.

Do not use animal, waterfowl, pond, quack or feather metaphors as product identity, technical terminology or interface decoration. Prefer clear functional names and explicit domain language.

The shared identity must never override accessibility, native platform behavior, security boundaries or each product's established information architecture.
