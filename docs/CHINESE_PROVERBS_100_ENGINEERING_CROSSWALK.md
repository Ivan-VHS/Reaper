## Review policy

- Treat each proverb as a mnemonic, not technical proof; validate claims against source, tests and CI.
- Use least privilege for capabilities and external interfaces.
- Irreversible operations require explicit authorization and rollback/recovery planning.
- Verify hardware, resource and security claims against platform specifications and measurements.
- Preserve defensive interpretation of offensive examples; do not implement attack tooling.
